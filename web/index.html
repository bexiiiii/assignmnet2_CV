Hand gesture recognition under changing conditions

Tokhtamishov Bekhruz · SE-2422 · Computer Vision, Assignment 2 · Astana IT University

Recognises three gestures: thumbs up, peace and stop. Trained on 450 frames of my own hand. Models: DINOv2 (whole image), MediaPipe hand landmarks with my own classifier, SAM 2.1 for the hand mask.

Consent

  • The server does not save the photo and does not use it for training.
  • The browser keeps your last photo in this tab so you can use it on the other pages. It is deleted when you close the tab or press Withdraw consent.
  • Only photograph your own hand, or someone who agreed. Try to keep faces out of the frame.
